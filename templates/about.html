About East Street Foundation

Our Story

East Street Foundation was created to help families take estate planning seriously before life forces difficult decisions. Our mission is to make wills, trusts, and legacy planning feel more approachable, organized, and understandable.

Our Mission

To help individuals and families protect their wishes, reduce confusion, and prepare important estate documents with confidence.

Why We Do This
